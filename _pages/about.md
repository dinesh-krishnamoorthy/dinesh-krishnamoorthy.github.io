<h2 class="ab-first">Short bio</h2>

<p>
Dinesh Krishnamoorthy is an Associate Professor at the <a href="https://www.ntnu.edu/itk">Department of Engineering Cybernetics</a>,
NTNU, where he leads the <a href="https://dinesh-krishnamoorthy.github.io/group/">COLLAB</a> research group. From 2022 to 2025 he was an Assistant Professor at the Department of Mechanical
Engineering at <a href="https://www.tue.nl/en/our-university/departments/mechanical-engineering/">TU Eindhoven</a>, in the
<a href="https://www.tue.nl/en/research/research-groups/control-systems-technology/">Control Systems Technology</a> group,
where he remains a Visiting Professor. From 2021 to 2022 he was a postdoctoral researcher at the Harvard John A. Paulson
School of Engineering and Applied Sciences.
</p>

<p>
He received his PhD in Process Systems Engineering from NTNU, his MSc in Control Systems from Imperial College London, and his
BEng in Mechatronics from the University of Nottingham. He also has more than four years of industrial research experience, as a
Senior Researcher at the Statoil Research Centre in Norway (2012–2016) and as a part-time Senior Data Science Consultant for
Novo Nordisk R&amp;D (2021).
</p>

<h2>Research interests</h2>

<p>
My research lies at the intersection of <b>optimization, control, and machine learning</b>, with a focus on
<b>collaborative decision making</b> in complex engineering systems: how many interacting subsystems or agents can
coordinate their decisions toward a common goal, safely and in real time, using only limited information.
</p>

<div class="ab-themes">
  <div class="ab-theme">
    <h3>Distributed optimization &amp; feedback-optimizing control</h3>
    <p>Decomposition-based algorithms  and feedback control structures that steer large-scale interconnected systems to optimal operation directly from measurements.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ab-theme">
    <h3>Model predictive control</h3>
    <p> Distributed,  mixed-integer, and learning-based MPC: learned controllers and value functions that keep MPC's guarantees.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ab-theme">
    <h3> Bayesian optimization for real-time decisions</h3>
    <p>Sample-efficient learning to optimize in real-time where constraint violations are not an option.</p>
  </div>
